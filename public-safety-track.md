# Public Safety / Law Enforcement Track

Use voluntary pilots and safety programs to reduce distracted driving.

## Buyer
- public agencies
- transportation safety groups
- school districts
- law-enforcement and enforcement-adjacent safety partners

## Offer
- pilot deployments
- aggregate reporting
- education materials
- research summaries

## Guardrail
Keep the pitch focused on safety, compliance, and prevention rather than punitive surveillance.
