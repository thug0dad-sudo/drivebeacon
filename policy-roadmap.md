# Policy Roadmap

## Goal
Build enough evidence to support safer-driving policies and optional public programs.

## Start with
- Fleet pilots
- Teen-driver programs
- School partnerships
- Insurance conversations
- Public-safety grants

## Later moves
- State pilot legislation
- Fleet procurement standards
- Teen-driver safety incentives
- Public awareness campaigns

## Important caution
Do not lead with a mandate-first pitch. Lead with data, pilots, and safety outcomes.

## Compliance themes
- Privacy
- Consent
- Vehicle lighting rules
- Liability
- Data retention
