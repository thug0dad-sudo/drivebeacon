# DriveBeacon Pitch Deck Outline

## 1. Title
DriveBeacon — safer driving, visible accountability

## 2. Problem
Distracted driving is common, hard to police, and expensive for families, fleets, and public agencies.

## 3. Solution
A privacy-first device that detects likely texting/distraction, warns the driver, logs events, and can trigger an external visible signal.

## 4. Why now
- distracted driving remains a major safety issue
- fleets already buy safety tech
- parents want accountability tools
- public agencies need pilot-ready solutions

## 5. Product
- driver notification
- event logging
- parent/fleet alerts
- external signal
- dashboard and summaries

## 6. Customers
- fleets first
- parents of teen drivers
- schools / safety programs
- public safety pilots

## 7. Business model
- hardware sale
- recurring software subscription
- install/support services

## 8. Differentiation
- external visible signal
- privacy-first defaults
- separate tracks for fleets, parents, and public safety
- coaching, not just detection

## 9. Go-to-market
- closed pilots
- case studies
- channel partners
- insurance and fleet distribution

## 10. Risks and mitigation
- false positives
- legal review
- privacy concerns
- lighting compliance

## 11. Ask
Pilot customers, research partners, and strategic introductions.
