# DriveBeacon One-Pager

## What it is
DriveBeacon is a distracted-driving safety platform that detects texting-like behavior, warns the driver, logs risk events, and supports fleet, parent, and public-safety use cases.

## Problem
Distracted driving is frequent, dangerous, and difficult to correct in real time.

## Solution
A privacy-first device and dashboard that create immediate feedback for the driver and optional accountability for parents, fleets, and safety partners.

## Market
- Fleet safety budgets
- Teen-driver families
- School and safety programs
- Public-safety pilots
