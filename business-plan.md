# DriveBeacon Business Plan

## 1. Problem
Distracted driving is common, hard to police, and costly for families, fleets, and public agencies.

## 2. Solution
A privacy-first device that detects likely texting/distraction, warns the driver, logs events, and can trigger an external visible signal.

## 3. Customers
- Fleet operators
- Parents of teen drivers
- Safety-focused organizations
- Public-safety / enforcement pilots

## 4. Value proposition
Reduce risky driving, improve accountability, and create real-world coaching data.

## 5. Product
- Driver notification
- Event logging
- Parent/fleet alerts
- External visible signal
- Dashboard and summaries

## 6. Business model
- Hardware sale
- Monthly SaaS per vehicle
- Install + support
- Pilot and enterprise contracts

## 7. Go-to-market
- Closed pilots
- Case studies
- Channel partnerships
- Insurance/fleet distribution

## 8. Risks
- False positives
- Privacy backlash
- Lighting/regulatory issues
- Liability if misused

## 9. Milestones
- Prototype
- Pilot
- Measured impact
- Compliance review
- Commercial launch
