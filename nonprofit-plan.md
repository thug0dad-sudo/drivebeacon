# DriveBeacon Nonprofit Plan

## Mission
Reduce distracted-driving harm through education, research, pilot programs, and policy support.

## Best legal lane
Likely a separate nonprofit entity for education and research, with a for-profit product company beside it.

## Activities
- Research briefs
- Teen-driver education
- Fleet safety pilots
- Public awareness campaigns
- Grants and partnerships

## Outputs
- Safety white papers
- Pilot data summaries
- Policy recommendations
- School/community toolkits

## Funding
- Donations
- Grants
- Sponsorships
- Program fees for research/pilots

## Guardrails
- Keep lobbying compliant
- Separate nonprofit and product finances
- Publish transparent safety data
