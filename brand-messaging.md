# Brand Messaging

## Positioning
DriveBeacon helps drivers stay safer through real-time alerts, logging, and accountability.

## Tone
- calm
- safety-first
- coaching-oriented
- privacy-respecting

## Avoid
- shame
- surveillance
- punishment
- gotcha language

## Core phrases
- safer driving
- visible accountability
- privacy-first coaching
- family and fleet safety
- real-time warnings

## Audience variants
- Fleet: reduce incidents and manage risk
- Parents: accountability and coaching
- Public safety: voluntary pilots and education
